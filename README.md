# SER-401_AI-Marketing-Agent
AI Marketing Tool

This project started as an AI Instagram poster: you feed it information about your brand, product, or event, and it generates a formatted Instagram post. The current demo implements this core flow.

From there, the team and our sponsor explored where an AI marketing tool could go next. The sponsor's vision is an influencer-focused tool that uses market research to recommend what content to capture, then formats the user's own photos into polished posts. Other directions the team proposed include:

A platform-specific application tailored to the conventions of a single social media channel
A research tool that studies popular creators and companies so users can emulate what works
Broader marketing support, including analysis of recurring feedback for smaller audiences
Location-aware market research that surfaces regional trends
A tool built primarily for small businesses without dedicated marketing teams

The long-term goal is to combine content generation with research-driven guidance, helping users decide both what to post and how to present it.
